
> **Purpose:** Capture the organization-specific procurement knowledge that the Procure AI Workspace must understand when supporting Etex vendor comparison.

> The source material describes two comparison contexts: **Direct comparison** and **Indirect comparison (IT)**. The workflows use different RFx templates and domain-specific evaluations, while both ultimately require a standardized, like-for-like comparison and a decision pack.

## 1. Core Etex Comparison Principle

The comparison process is built around **standardized supplier inputs** so that offers can be evaluated on a like-for-like basis.

For both comparison contexts, the standardized matrix combines areas such as:

```text
Commercial
Delivery
Risk
Compliance
Quality
Technical
Stakeholder Evaluation
```

and supports single-year and multi-year TCO analysis.
The AI therefore needs to understand:

> **The comparison matrix is not merely an output spreadsheet; it represents the structured evaluation model of the procurement exercise.**

---

# 2. Etex Direct Comparison Domain

## RFx Knowledge

The direct comparison process may involve:

* RFI / RFP
* Requirements Checklist
* Q&A Document
* Pricing Template
* Assumptions Template
* Risk Plan
* Obligations Template
* Technical Assessment
* Safety Assessment
* NDA
* Personal Data Protection Questionnaire
* Frame Agreement / Contract
* SLA where applicable

> These documents establish the sourcing scope, mandatory requirements, pricing structure, assumptions, technical and safety evaluations, and contractual context.

# 3. Etex Indirect / IT Comparison Domain

The IT comparison process uses a similar core structure but contains IT-specific knowledge.

Relevant RFx inputs include:

* RFI / RFP
* Requirements Checklist
* Q&A
* Pricing Template
* Assumptions
* Risk Plan
* Obligations
* Architecture Assessment
* Security Assessment / EVSAT
* NDA
* Personal Data Protection Questionnaire
* Services Contract / MSA
* DPA where required

> The IT process therefore extends the common procurement model with architecture, security, GDPR/privacy, and service-specific concerns.

# 4. Etex Requirement Knowledge

Requirements are represented through structured checklists and can include:

```text
Functional
Technical
Service
Operational
Commercial
Security
Compliance
Safety
```

Vendors may explicitly indicate:

```text
YES
NO
PARTIAL
```

and provide clarification.

This creates a direct mapping between:

```text
Requirement
    ↕
Vendor Response
    ↕
Gap / Deviation
```

The requirements checklist is explicitly intended to support quick validation of scope fit and identification of gaps or deviations.

```text

+
```

# 10. Risk & Assumption Knowledge

The AI needs to identify:

* Assumptions
* Exclusions
* Dependencies
* Etex obligations
* Delivery risks
* Commercial risks
* Compliance deviations
* Scope gaps

A particularly important concept is:

> **Scope certainty versus assumptions.**

The comparison should determine how much of the proposed scope is clearly committed versus dependent on assumptions or exclusions.

# 19. Expected AI Outputs

The supplied domain definition makes two core business artifacts explicit:

### Comparison Matrix

The Excel comparison sheet is the **source of truth** for structured inputs such as costs, scoring, assumptions, risks, dependencies, and stakeholder scores.

### PowerPoint Decision Pack

The PPT is the management/SteerCo visualization layer derived systematically from the Excel model. Numbers, scores, rankings, and conclusions in the presentation must be traceable back to the Excel model.

A typical structure includes:

```text
Cover
Vendor Overview
Selection Approach
Scoring Model
Summary Scoring
Pricing
Internal Cost / TCO
Pricing Deep Dive
Key Takeaways
Ranking Rationale
```

---

## Domain Principle

> **For Etex, vendor comparison is a structured, evidence-backed evaluation of scope, commercial value, technical and operational fit, risk, and long-term TCO — with the Excel comparison model acting as the structured source of truth and the PowerPoint acting as the business decision layer.**
